## Decisions made in this plan

### Overbet defence (Task 1)
- **New probes:** at each Hero unopened post-flop decision (where `_probe_records` probes 33/75/100%), also probe **2× pot** and **all-in**. They share the existing `seen` set, so at low SPR, where `bet_to(1.0)` or `bet_to(2.0)` already clamps to all-in (`ALLIN_FRACTION`), each distinct `Action` is probed once. They are called with `defence_weight=0.0`, so they never feed the per-street `defence_*` checks.
- **`defence_overbet` check:** Villain's continue rate (call + raise) divided by MDF = 1/(1+f), where f = bet / pot before the bet. It uses only probes whose bet is between **2× and 3× pot inclusive**: the 2× probe, and the all-in probe when it lands in that window. Each qualifying probe is one record of weight 1 that counts toward `samples` (Hero's TAG policy never overbets, so there is no Hero frequency to weight by). Band: the profile's `defence_ratio`. Rationale: near 2–3× pot, MDF (0.25–0.33) is a meaningful target; against a 20×-pot jam MDF is about 0.05, and NUTS (never folds) plus STRONG (floored, below) alone exceed it, so huge jams are not a defence target.
- **Huge jams** (all-in above 3× pot) feed only `nuts_fold_max`, `strong_fold_max` and the river `air_*` checks, which already take every probe (`_facing_records`).
- **STRONG floor:** after size scaling, STRONG continues **≥ 0.90** facing any bet size. This is distinct from invariant 8 (STRONG's unscaled `facing_bet` table row continues ≥ 0.93), which still holds. `strong_fold_max` (≤ 0.10) enforces the floor for bets of pot or more.
- **How `_facing` changes** is the implementer's call (Opus), within these constraints: NUTS never folds; the STRONG floor above; MEDIUM and WEAK continue less than today against bets above pot; behaviour against bets of pot or less changes as little as possible (the existing `defence_flop/turn/river` ratios stay in band). Document the formula in the `_facing` docstring.
- **Strategy unit test, fixed spot:** heads-up, BTN opens to 5, BB calls, then a scripted check/bet line to the river on a named monotone board (the implementer picks and names the board and line in the test). Villain's range is that line's `RangeTracker` posterior for the profile. Facing a Hero bet of 2.5× pot there, the whole-range continue share is within `defence_ratio` × MDF. Separately, on any board, STRONG continues ≥ 0.90 at 1×, 2.5× and 20× pot, and NUTS continues 1.0.

### Pre-flop: pause BB-vs-open puzzles, add a 3-bet defence check (Task 2)
- **Why pause:** the plan review computed that BTN's defence against Hero's 3-bet is already near MDF for TAG, LAG and Tight. Yet 3-betting 32o/53o/75o from the BB still grades as +EV by 0.47–0.86 bb, and BTN would need to continue about 67% (outside TAG's band) to stop it. The cause is the grader: a called pre-flop 3-bet (or call) is valued as if the out-of-position BB realises its full equity (`realisation`). Villain tuning can't fix that. **The user's ruling: pause these puzzles until the M9 one-street lookahead fixes the grading.**
- **How:** `puzzle/config.json` gains `"paused_spots": ["preflop_bb_vs_open"]` (bump config `version` to 4; the loader in `puzzle/model.py` validates the list against known spot names). In `generate_spot`, when Hero would stop pre-flop as the BB facing a non-all-in open (exactly one pre-flop action, a raise by the button, and Hero can still raise), and that spot is paused, return `None` (the generator retries). The decision-point draw and every other spot are unaffected. Keep the filter in one small named helper so M9 can delete it.
- **Other pre-flop spots stay:** BTN first in, BTN facing a 3-bet (Hero in position), BB facing a jam (all-in, no realisation issue).
- **`defence_preflop_3bet` check:** computed from the profile's pre-flop ranges, no simulation. Continue = Σ open·(btn_call_threebet + btn_jam) / Σ open over the 1,326 combos (no card removal; stated in the docstring). It is compared with the MDF formula in `handoff.md` §6: MDF = 1/(1+f), f = call / (pot before the call − call), for both 3-bet sizes Hero is offered facing the 2.5× open. Get the `to` amounts from `raise_to` on a real state (they are 12 and 15: f = 0.7, MDF ≈ 0.588; f = 1, MDF = 0.5). The check passes when **both** ratios are inside the profile's `defence_ratio` band. It's one deterministic sample per profile: give it `samples = 1`, and exempt it from `MIN_SAMPLES` by name, with a comment (don't lower `MIN_SAMPLES`).
- The reviewer's numbers (continue share; ratios at 12 / 15): TAG 0.569 (0.97 / 1.14, passes), LAG 0.584 (0.99 / 1.17, passes), Tight 0.462 (0.79 / 0.92, passes), **Sticky 0.688 (1.17 / 1.38, fails: over-defends)**. In-band windows: TAG [0.50, 0.575], LAG [0.50, 0.60], Tight [0.41, 0.50], Sticky [0.59, 0.675]. **Tune Sticky only** (narrow `btn_call_threebet`, keep `btn_jam` value-only, invariant 7) to about the middle of its window; bump Sticky's version. Leave the others alone.
- **`defence_preflop_open`** (BB continue against the 2.5× open, `bb_call + bb_threebet` over all 1,326 combos, divided by MDF): printed as info only.

### Grader and API (Tasks 3, 4)
- **Custom size beats every preset:** `grade()` takes `best` as the chosen custom option when its EV is strictly higher than the preset best by more than `_EPS`; otherwise the preset best. EV lost is 0 and the verdict "best", as today, but `Grade.best` (and so the stored `best_label`, `best_kind`, `best_ev_chips`) is the custom option. The result view marks the appended custom option `is_best` and the preset best `is_best: false` in that case. It decides from the stored EVs (custom chosen EV vs `sol.options[sol.best]` EV, same `_EPS`), so old attempts render consistently without regrading. Old attempts keep their stored preset `best_label` (accepted; recorded).
- **Custom `best_label` is in chips** ("bet 14", from `solve._label_for`), and history returns it raw. Leave the stored label as is. The frontend already relabels a custom chosen option in bb; in Task 3 make sure the result's best label shown anywhere goes through the same bb relabelling (`lib/result.ts`). Record for M7: history's `best_label` for a custom best is in chips and must be relabelled when M7 shows it.
- **Unknown profile in a stored puzzle:** a helper in `api/views.py` returns the profile's display text, falling back to `label = name.upper()` and `description = "This Villain profile is no longer available."` when `load_profile` raises `ValueError`. `puzzle_view`, `attempt_result` and `villain_model_current` use it; `villain_model_current` returns `False` for an unknown profile (so custom sizes get the existing 409 at `service.py` `_resolve_action`, and `custom.allowed` is false). `reveal.profile.version` keeps coming from the stored puzzle.
