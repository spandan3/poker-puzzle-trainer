## Decisions made in this plan

### Engine (`game/state.py`)
- **`HandState` gets no new fields.** Per-seat fields become `tuple[int, ...]` of length n (n = `len(stacks)`).
  - Folded seats come from `history` (seats with a `FOLD`). A helper `folded_seats(s)` returns them.
  - `folded` keeps its meaning: None until the hand ends by folds, and then the seat whose fold ended it. So heads-up is unchanged, and so is `is_terminal`.
  - Contributions are `start_stacks[i] - s.stacks[i]`. Every caller that needs them (settlement, frames' pots) has the record's `start_stacks`.
- **`new_hand(button, stacks)`**, with n = `len(stacks)`:
  - Two seats: exactly today's rule.
  - Three or more: SB is `button+1` and posts 1; BB is `button+2` and posts 2. The first to act is `button+3` mod n.
  - With n ≥ 3, assert every stack is greater than BB. The session rules guarantee it (Hero ≥ 10, bots ≥ 80), so a blind posted all-in can't happen. Random tests use stacks ≥ 3.
- **Bet cap (`legal_actions`):** `max_to = min(own stack + own bet, max over other live seats not all-in of (their stack + their bet))`. With two seats this equals today's effective-stack cap. If no other seat can act, the seat can only call or fold.
- **Next actor:** the next seat clockwise that hasn't folded and isn't all-in.
  - The round ends when every such seat has acted on this street and matches the highest bet, or when no such seat remains, or one remains and it has matched.
  - Post-flop the first actor is the first live, not-all-in seat clockwise from the button.
- **Reopening (ruling):** a seat that has already acted may re-raise only if a full raise came after its last action (`_can_reraise`, generalised). This is a simplification of the TDA's cumulative rule, and heads-up is identical either way.
- **`deal`:** as today. The run-out happens when fewer than two seats can still bet.
- **Helpers:**
  - `position_name(s, seat)`: heads-up as today; 5 seats per Global Constraints; 3, 4 and 6 seats by the standard names (tests only).
  - `live_seats(s)` and `active_seats(s)` (live and not all-in).
  - `last_aggressor` is unchanged.
- **The puzzle model is unchanged:** `_state_to_dict` and `_state_from_dict` stay as they are, because no field is added.

### Settlement (`game/settle.py`)
- **`settle(s, holes) -> tuple[int, ...]`** keeps its signature and its heads-up results (`tests/game/test_settle.py` is unchanged). It is generalised to n seats by calling `settle_pots` with contributions taken from the state.
  - Heads-up may keep its current code path, so its results stay identical.
  - With n ≥ 3, `settle` requires `start_stacks`, so callers use `settle_pots` directly.
- **New: `settle_pots(s, holes: Sequence[tuple[int, int] | None], start_stacks: Sequence[int]) -> Settlement`**, where `Settlement(stacks, pots: tuple[PotAward, ...])` and `PotAward(amount, eligible, winners)`.
  - Layers come from the distinct contribution levels. A layer's amount is the sum over all seats of `min(c, level) - min(c, prev)`. Its eligible seats are the live seats with `c >= level`.
  - A top layer with one eligible seat is an uncalled bet: it goes back to that seat's stack and is not listed.
  - Each listed pot goes to the best `evaluate` among its eligible seats. A tie splits evenly, and odd chips go one at a time to the winners in clockwise order from `button+1`.
  - If only one live seat remains, it takes everything as one listed pot.
- **Reference test:** copy today's `game/state.py` and `game/settle.py` to `backend/tests/reference/hu_engine.py` (the existing `tests/reference` convention, `pythonpath = ["tests"]`). Repoint the imports inside the copy, and add a header saying it's frozen.
  - A property test (seeded random, at least 3,000 hands, stacks 2–600) drives both engines with the same legal choices.
  - At every step it asserts equal `legal_actions`, `to_act`, stacks, bets, pot and history (converting between the two `Action` classes), and at the end the same settled stacks.

### Pre-flop bots, 5-max (`villain/preflop5.py`, new)
- **Data:**
  - `backend/src/poker_trainer/villain/hand_ranking.json`: `{"version": 1, "classes": [...169 labels, strongest first...]}`. It is generated by a script `poker_trainer.analysis.hand_ranking` (all-in equity against a random hand, 50,000 samples per class, seed `random.Random("hand-ranking:<label>")`). Ties are broken by label so the order is stable. Both files are loaded with `importlib.resources`, like `categories.json`.
  - `backend/src/poker_trainer/villain/fivemax.json`: `{"version": 1, "bb_call_mult": 1.6, "damping": {"bluff": 0.5, "thin": 0.6, "call": 0.8}, "profiles": {"tag": {...}, ...}}`. Each profile has `open: {UTG, CO, BTN, SB}`, `vs_open: {threebet, call}`, `vs_threebet: {jam, call}` and `vs_jam: {call}`, all as percentages of the 1326 combos. Starting values (a range of 0–100%):

    | profile | UTG | CO | BTN | SB | 3-bet | call | jam vs 3b | call vs 3b | call vs jam |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | tight | 11 | 18 | 30 | 26 | 4 | 7 | 2.5 | 4 | 3 |
    | tag | 14 | 24 | 40 | 34 | 6 | 9 | 3 | 6 | 4 |
    | lag | 19 | 32 | 52 | 45 | 9 | 12 | 4 | 9 | 5 |
    | sticky | 16 | 27 | 45 | 40 | 4 | 18 | 2.5 | 12 | 6 |

  - A bucket is a slice of the combos in ranking order. A class that straddles a bucket boundary gets a fractional weight, so the percentages are exact. "3-bet t%, call c%" means the top t% 3-bet and the next c% call.
  - The big blind's call % is `call × bb_call_mult`, capped so the 3-bet and call buckets together are at most 100%.
- **Spot by number of raises** (`r` = the number of `RAISE` actions pre-flop). The pre-flop history may contain `FOLD`, `CALL` and `RAISE`. A `CHECK` can't happen, because without limps the big blind never gets an option: a walk ends the hand.
  - **Facing a jam** means: `lg.aggressive is None`, or any pre-flop raiser now has stack 0, or `r >= 3`. Then the only choices are fold or call, with `vs_jam.call`. This holds for bots and for Hero (I2 of the review), and it makes every later spot fold/call, so `UnsupportedSpot` can't happen.
  - `r == 0`: fold or open. The size is `raise_to(s, 2.5)`, or `raise_to(s, 3.0)` from the SB (3 bb: `raise_to` multiplies the 2-chip bet faced; the original 1.5 was a typo, corrected in Task 2). Opening percentages come from the seat's position.
  - `r == 1` (and not facing a jam): fold, call, or 3-bet to `_sized(s, lg, RAISE, round((3 + callers) * max(street_bets)))`, where `callers` is the number of `CALL`s after the open.
  - `r == 2` (and not facing a jam): fold, call or jam (`allin`).
- `action_distribution(profile, s)` routes pre-flop states with exactly five seats to `preflop5`, keyed by `profile.name` in `fivemax.json`. 3, 4 and 6 seats, or an unknown profile name, raise `UnsupportedSpot`. Heads-up keeps `_preflop`.
- Hero's options (`puzzle/options.py`) with five seats, using the same facing-a-jam test (checked first). The heads-up branch at `options.py:35` (any non-RAISE is rejected) stays for two seats:
  - `r == 0`: fold, raise 2.5x, raise 3x, all-in;
  - `r == 1`: fold, call, raise 2.5x / 3x, all-in;
  - `r == 2`: fold, call, all-in;
  - a jam faced: fold, call.

  Heads-up keeps today's branches byte for byte.

### Post-flop bots, multiway (`villain/strategy.py`)
- `role(s)` already works for N seats. Keep it, and add tests for 3+ seats: c-bet, lead, stab, facing bet, facing raise.
- `street_spr(s)` (`villain/features.py`): `min(own stack + bet, max over other live seats of (stack + bet)) / pot`. Heads-up gives the same value; pin it.
- **Damping happens inside the tables.** `_unopened` and `_facing` take `k` = (live opponents − 1), counting all-in opponents. Bluffs still have to get through an all-in player's main pot. They damp the `(8, k)` category matrix `m` before actions are built and merged:
  - In `_unopened`, after the river override and the draw boost: the bet columns of `AIR`, `WEAK_DRAW` and `WEAK` are multiplied by `bluff^k`, and the bet columns of `MEDIUM` by `thin^k`. The removed mass goes to check. `STRONG_DRAW`, `STRONG` and `NUTS` are not damped.
  - In `_facing`, after the continue curve and before the low-SPR step:
    - `MEDIUM`'s raise moves to call;
    - the raise column of `AIR`, `WEAK_DRAW` and `WEAK` is multiplied by `bluff^k`;
    - then the call and raise of every row below `STRONG` (`AIR`, `WEAK_DRAW`, `WEAK`, `MEDIUM`, `STRONG_DRAW`) are multiplied by `call^k`;
    - all removed mass goes to fold.

    When `street_spr <= 1`, the `_COMMITTED` rows are skipped, and the low-SPR step then runs as today. `STRONG` and `NUTS` are untouched.
  - `k = 0` is a no-op by construction. The constants come from `fivemax.json` `damping`. The spec is updated to name `WEAK` among the bluff rows and the bluff factor on raises.
- `STRATEGY_VERSION` stays 1 (Global Constraints). `docs/villain.md` gains the rule: 5-max changes bump `fivemax.json` `version` or `hand_ranking.json` `version`, not `STRATEGY_VERSION`.
- `narrowing.describe` works for N seats (it reads only the acting seat). Add a test.

- `outcome5(r)` (5 seats) returns `HandOutcome5(ended_by, winner, stacks, hero_delta, pots: tuple[PotAward, ...], shown: tuple[int, ...], hero_folded: bool)`. `winner` is "villain" when the hand was abandoned or Hero folded; otherwise it is the sign of `hero_delta`: hero, villain or split, as heads-up showdowns do (Task 4 review). `shown` is the seats whose cards are revealed: the live seats at a showdown, Hero excluded, clockwise from button+1 (the button last). `hero_folded` is True when Hero has a fold in the history, even if the bots went on to a showdown.   A fast version runs 200 hands.

11. The N-seat functions are `advance_seats` and `hero_act_seats`, and the heads-up names are kept (I9).

## Follow-up during the build

### Task 1

Rulings accepted in the Task 1 review:

- `settle` rebuilds each seat's contribution from the history for three or more seats. Play and API code should call `settle_pots` with the record's `start_stacks` instead.
- Adjacent layers with the same eligible seats are merged. The plan's "layers" means the pots after merging.
- A layer with one live eligible seat but several payers is a listed pot. Only single-payer layers are uncalled bets that go back.
- The heads-up reference engine is one frozen module.
- `_can_reraise` uses `any()` over the full raises since the seat's last action. Heads-up it is identical to the old rule.

### Task 2

Commit `c58c26f`. The Opus review approved it with no blockers and no important findings. Its fuzz run covered about 180k pre-flop decisions, at stacks from 3 to 700 chips, with no `UnsupportedSpot`, no limp states and no illegal actions.

**Rulings:**
- **The SB opens with `raise_to(s, 3.0)`,** which is 3 bb. The plan's 1.5 was a typo.
- **Two mutants are kept as equivalent:** the BB call cap (`bucket` clamps anyway) and counting callers from the first raise.

**Deferred to the final fix wave (minor):**
- **Stricter `fivemax.json` validation** (`preflop5.py:117-134`): a missing key should give `ValueError`, unknown keys should be rejected, `version` and `bb_call_mult` need type checks, and the profile names should be checked against `PROFILE_NAMES` at load.
- **`hand_ranking.py`:** the module docstring should say the output depends on CPython's `random.sample` (one uv lock).

**Open items, for M14 tuning:**
- **The ranking ignores playability.** Equity against a random hand ranks small pairs and offsuit broadway-ish hands above suited connectors. For example, TAG opens J9o and K8o on the BTN but not 22 or 76s. Close neighbours are also in sampling-noise order (±0.2%).
- **Hero's 3-bet sizes** with callers are 2.5x / 3x of the open, while bots squeeze to (3 + callers) × open. Consider adding the bot size to Hero's menu.
- **`docs/villain.md`** needs a 5-max section (Task 10).
